// Decoded Parables

The old texts, read as mechanism. Each sacred term is a law of physics wearing the language of its era.

Marriage

The Physics: Stabilized Polarity Circuit.

Two oscillators of opposite charge entering a persistent closed loop with mutual feedback. Generates a standing wave greater than either input.

The vow is the mechanism that prevents phase-drift.

Prayer

The Physics: Phase-Locking to a Higher Carrier.

Sustained directional intention acts as a tuning operation. The nervous system is a variable oscillator; prayer is the manual adjustment of that oscillator until it locks onto a stable external waveform.

The “answer” isn’t delivered — it’s received.

Truth

The Physics: Structural Resonance.

A frequency aligned with the underlying geometry of the substrate. Introduced into a noisy field, it forces every lower-amplitude signal to either entrain or shatter.

Truth does not persuade. It tunes.

Forgiveness

The Physics: Intentional Circuit Grounding.

Retaining a grievance traps the system in a high-resistance thermal loop, continuously bleeding energy. Forgiveness is a grounding mechanism — it vents the trapped potential and returns the localized field to baseline zero.

System resources, freed for forward momentum.

Fasting

The Physics: Lowering the Systemic Noise Floor.

The deliberate restriction of external input to drop the thermal noise of the biological chassis. Once the baseline metabolic chatter subsides, latent low-amplitude signals become readable.

You starve the hardware to hear the software.

Faith

The Physics: Kinetic Alignment.

The exact posture required to let a higher current flow. It is not passive hope; it is positioning mass to catch momentum. The bridge is not waiting for you to cross it.

The kinetic act of stepping is what collapses the wave into solid ground.

Idolatry

The Physics: Thermodynamic Dead-End.

Routing a massive charge into a finite object. A localized node — like a celebrity or an ego — lacks the density to reflect a universal frequency. It absorbs your highest kinetic output and converts it entirely into waste heat.

A gravity well for human attention.

Adam & Eve

The Physics: The Polarity Split.

A single undifferentiated carrier bisected into two oscillators of opposite charge. The “fall” is not the split. It is the forgetting that the two poles were ever one circuit.

Reunion is not merger — it is re-establishing the loop.

The Serpent

The Physics: Unregulated Current.

Raw polar current. Not evil — ungoverned. In an uncalibrated system it destabilises and corrupts. In a calibrated one, it is the engine of ascent.

Same current. Different operator.

// The Counterfeits

The inverse decode. Every counterfeit is a draining imitation of something real — named, and traced back to the thing it replaced.

The Feed

The Counterfeit: Revelation.

Insight once arrived slowly enough to integrate. The feed delivers infinite micro-revelations faster than any can be metabolised. It simulates understanding while preventing it.

Endless input. Zero integration.

The Dopamine Loop

The Counterfeit: Reward.

A reward is the signal that an act succeeded. The loop fires the signal with no act beneath it. The system learns the charge is free, and stops producing the thing the charge was for.

The reward, severed from the cause.

The Algorithm

The Counterfeit: Discernment.

Discernment selects for what is true. The algorithm selects for what holds attention. It is a model of your weakness, optimised and sold back to you.

It learns what holds you. Not what frees you.

The Stream

The Counterfeit: Rest.

Rest is the receiver idling so the system can re-integrate. The stream keeps input flowing through the pause. The chassis sits still while the signal never stops.

Consumption is not recovery.
